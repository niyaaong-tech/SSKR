# HOME Hero Asset

## `hero-sunrise-drawing-v01.png`

- 제작 방식: Codex built-in `image_gen`
- 용도: HOME Intro의 background key visual
- 지형 에셋: 사용하지 않음
- UI / 타이포그래피: 이미지에 포함하지 않음

### Final prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: premium website landing-page hero background for a Korean motorcycle rally
Primary request: Draw a cinematic sunrise over the East Sea with a real sense of ocean, first light, sky, and clouds — richer and more tactile than a flat color gradient, but still elegant enough to sit behind large editorial typography.
Scene/backdrop: open sea at dawn viewed from a low coastal horizon; long calm ocean surface with delicate ripples and a narrow trail of reflected sunlight; layered high clouds and low mist catching peach light; no coastline, mountains, islands, terrain, buildings, boats, or people.
Style/medium: sophisticated hand-painted digital illustration with restrained cinematic realism, subtle paper/film grain, atmospheric depth, visible soft brush texture; not photorealistic stock photography and not a glossy 3D render.
Composition/framing: very wide 16:9 landscape; horizon around the lower 72%; large dark-blue negative space across the upper-left and center-left for white website copy; sun low near the horizon in the right third, not centered; clouds remain soft and do not interfere with text.
Lighting/mood: just before sunrise becoming first light, quiet anticipation, cool navy and blue-gray above transitioning to muted apricot, amber, and pale gold near the horizon.
Color palette: deep ink navy, blue-gray, dusty mauve, muted apricot, warm amber, pale gold; restrained saturation.
Materials/textures: fine cloud strata, marine haze, gentle water ripples, soft solar glow and reflection, subtle painterly grain.
Constraints: background art only; no text, no typography, no logo, no watermark, no interface, no motorcycle, no land or terrain; horizon must read clearly as ocean; preserve generous dark negative space on the left.
```

### Final prompt

```text
Create a wide 16:9 painterly digital illustration for a premium Korean cycling event website background. Scene: the West Sea at sunset, viewed straight across an open ocean with a long perfectly horizontal horizon line around 64% down the frame. No land, no coastline, no islands, no mountains, no terrain, no boats, no people. Deep plum and navy upper sky transitioning through muted coral and burnt orange near the horizon, with layered atmospheric clouds and subtle sea haze. Warm afterglow is strongest at the far left horizon, leaving a luminous open gap there for a separate animated HTML sun disc to descend into the sea; do not draw the sun itself. Calm dark water below with restrained horizontal reflections and a tactile, slightly grainy editorial illustration finish, elegant and cinematic, consistent with a sophisticated scroll-narrative microsite. Composition should remain quiet behind white typography and polaroid cards. No text, no logos, no watermark, no UI elements.
```
